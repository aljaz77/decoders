**Senstick KOU20**

A long-probe LoRaWAN sensor for compost, soil, and mulch temperature monitoring at waste management sites.

**Key features:**

- LoRaWAN end-to-end encryption and data integrity protection
- Temperature probe accuracy (±1°C)
- Built in accelerometer for movement detection
- Up to 7 years of battery life
- IP68 rated aluminum probe casing for harsh environments
- Custom probe lengths available on request
- Easily replaceable AA battery
- Can be configured remotely or locally with Senzemo app
