**Senspuck Pure SPU30**

A LoRaWAN indoor air quality sensor for monitoring CO2, TVOC, temperature, relative humidity, and air pressure.

**Key features:**

- LoRaWAN end-to-end encryption and data integrity protection
- CO2 sensor accuracy (±30ppm)
- TVOC sensor accuracy (±0.5mg/m3)
- Temperature sensor accuracy (±0.2°C)
- Humidity sensor accuracy (±1.8%)
- Air pressure sensor accuracy (±1hPa)
- Up to 7 years of battery life without TVOC logging
- Default sending interval of 12 minutes, configurable via downlink
- Li-Ion battery for long-term autonomous operation
