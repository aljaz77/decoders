**Senstick Rainmeter SRM10**

A LoRaWAN tipping-bucket rain gauge for accurate rainfall metering in agriculture and hydrology applications.

**Key features:**

- LoRaWAN end-to-end encryption and data integrity protection
- High accuracy rainfall metering (±2%), 0.2mm per tip
- Built in leakage alert
- Built in manipulation and tamper alert
- Up to 10 years of battery life
- IP67 rated enclosure for outdoor use
- Easily replaceable single AA battery
- Optional metal pole holder and bird/leaf protector accessories
- Can be configured remotely or locally with Senzemo app
