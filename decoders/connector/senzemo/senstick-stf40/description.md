**Senstick STF40**

A LoRaWAN food probe sensor for food storage and cooking temperature monitoring.

**Key features:**

- LoRaWAN end-to-end encryption and data integrity protection
- Temperature probe accuracy (±0.5°C) across a very wide range, including active cooking conditions
- Built in accelerometer for movement detection
- Up to 7 years of battery life
- IP68 rated probe and enclosure
- Optional snap-on mounting kit
- Easily replaceable AA lithium battery
- Can be configured remotely or locally with Senzemo app
