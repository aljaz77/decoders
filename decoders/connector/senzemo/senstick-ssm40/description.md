**Senstick Soil Moisture SSM40**

A LoRaWAN probe sensor for monitoring volumetric water content in agriculture and irrigation applications.

**Key features:**

- LoRaWAN end-to-end encryption and data integrity protection
- Volumetric water content sensing across full moisture range
- Built in accelerometer for movement detection
- Up to 7 years of battery life
- IP68 rated probe and enclosure for underground and outdoor use
- Optional pairing with METER EC-5 soil probe for higher precision
- Easily replaceable AA lithium battery
- Can be configured remotely or locally with Senzemo app
