**Senstick Microclimate SMC40**

A compact LoRaWAN sensor for indoor and outdoor deployment, monitoring temperature, relative humidity, and air pressure in a slimmer single-battery form factor.

**Key features:**

- LoRaWAN end-to-end encryption and data integrity protection
- High accuracy temperature sensor (±0.2°C)
- Humidity sensor accuracy (±1.8%)
- Air pressure sensor accuracy (±1hPa)
- Built in accelerometer for movement and tampering detection
- Up to 7 years of battery life
- IP65 rated enclosure for harsh environments
- Easily replaceable single AA battery
- Can be configured remotely or locally with Senzemo app
