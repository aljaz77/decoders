**Senstick Ambient SAM20**

A reliable, easy-to-deploy ambient LoRaWAN sensor for monitoring temperature, relative humidity, and air pressure in sensitive indoor environments such as museums, archives, HVAC systems, server rooms, and data centers.

**Key features:**

- LoRaWAN end-to-end encryption and data integrity protection
- Monitors temperature, relative humidity, and air pressure
- Built in tampering detection
- Up to 7 years of battery life with a single AA alkaline battery
- IP20 rated enclosure (indoor use)
- NFC or over-the-air reconfiguration
- Internal data logging up to 40 days (prevents data loss on weak connections)
- Can be configured remotely or locally with Senzemo app
