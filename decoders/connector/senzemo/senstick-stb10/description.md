**Senstick STB10**

A dual-channel LoRaWAN food simulant sensor that reduces false alarms in cold chain and HACCP monitoring by mimicking real product thermal behavior.

**Key features:**

- LoRaWAN end-to-end encryption and data integrity protection
- Ambient temperature sensor accuracy (±0.2°C)
- Buffered product temperature sensor accuracy (±0.2°C), via lab-grade glass bead thermal mass
- Built in accelerometer for movement and tampering detection
- Up to 7 years of battery life
- Internal data logging up to 40 days (prevents data loss on weak connections)
- IP67 rated enclosure
- Easily replaceable AA alkaline battery
- Can be configured remotely or locally with Senzemo app
